What the feedback said

We analyzed 10 peer comments. None were negative, and most combined praise with one specific suggestion. The suggestions fell into four themes, and each refinement below is labeled with the theme it addresses.

10peer comments analyzed
80%positive (1 pure praise, 7 praise + suggestion)
0negative comments (1 mixed, 1 neutral)
Upcyclingthe most-praised topic (4 comments)

Clarity & visual cues

Make trades, steps and choices obvious to first-time players. (3 comments)

Learning built into play

Explain mistakes as they happen and put short facts on cards. (2 comments)

Fairer mechanics & decision rules

Allow partial progress and give groups a way to settle disagreements. (2 comments)

Age-appropriate language

Keep words and concepts at the right level for the audience. (1 comment)

The games

Each card shows the original rules and the refined logic. Refinements tied to a specific peer comment show its number. The others apply a theme from the feedback as a whole. Rules the reviewers praised are marked kept.

Play the demo: Recycling Rush, refined

This demo applies feedback comment #1: when you sort something into the wrong bin, the game tells you why straight away and pauses the clock while you read. Next time that item appears, the correct bin glows once. Rules vary by city, so check what your local service accepts.
